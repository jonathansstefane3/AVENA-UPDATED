Our story

A restaurant that started with a bag of oats and a stone mill.

Why grains

AVENA began with a simple question: why do grains so rarely get to be the main event? Oats, barley, spelt and millet have fed people for thousands of years, yet most menus keep them in the background.

We built the kitchen around them. Every dish starts with a grain and every other ingredient is chosen to support it.

How we cook

  • We mill our own flour

    Whole grains are ground in the kitchen each week, so bread and pasta keep their flavour and their bran.

  • We bake at dawn

    Oat bread comes out of the oven before the doors open. When it sells out, it sells out.

  • We buy from small farms

    Our grains, vegetables and dairy come from growers we know by name, and the menu changes with their harvests.
