AVENA

Avena is the Latin name for oats. We cook with oats, barley, spelt and millet, milled and baked in house every morning.

A kitchen built around the grain

Most restaurants treat grains as a side dish. At AVENA they set the direction of the whole menu. We toast, ferment and slow-cook them until each one tastes like itself.

We buy from small farms, mill our own flour and bake bread at dawn. Read our story.
